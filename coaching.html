Coaching

Life and career coaching

I help people through job searches, career changes and big decisions. I changed careers myself, from the kitchen to IT, and I have run my own job search, so I keep things practical.

What we can work on

Your career story

Turn your experience into a clear story for your CV, your LinkedIn profile and the first five minutes of an interview.

Interview preparation

Mock interviews, structuring your answers, and speaking clearly under pressure.

Career changes

Moving to a new role, industry or level, and working out which of your skills carry over.

Decisions that matter

Weighing options, setting priorities and agreeing the next step you will actually take.

I coach and advise. I do not promise a job or any other outcome, because that depends on you and the market. What I can do is help you prepare well and decide well.
