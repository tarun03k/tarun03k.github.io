GENAI AND AGENTIC ENGINEERING

AI for the software development lifecycle

Rolled out an on-prem GenAI coding assistant to thousands of developers in three gated phases, then added multi-model orchestration, AI pull-request review and agentic tooling.

  • Release gates for security, privacy and output quality
  • An agent harness that routes requests to cut cloud model cost
  • AI agents that automate RAID logs and status reports
95% adoption 25% PR throughput 30% lower model cost 40% less reporting time
RETAIL TRANSFORMATION

AI-powered store replenishment

Led a cross-functional team delivering demand-led replenishment for store colleagues: wearable devices, data science optimisers and store operations on one roadmap, with readiness gates from trial to MVP to rollout.

  • Hardware and software integration over Wi-Fi, BLE, REST and MDM
  • Device and trial funding brought forward by a year with senior approval
  • Targeting a 50% cut in inventory waste
10% colleague productivity 25% more colleague availability
PLATFORM ENGINEERING

Developer platform modernisation

Moved mission-critical Tier-0 and Tier-1 platforms to OpenShift, replaced static CI runners with auto-scaling ones, and put software supply-chain governance across thousands of repositories.

  • SBOM and licence compliance from proof of concept to MVP on 16K+ repos
  • About 500 pipelines moved to dynamic runners
  • Tooling held at 99.9% availability
Job wait 4 hrs to 1 hr 25% less operational overhead
DATA SCIENCE AND AI

LLM products and an enterprise RAG chatbot

At Wolters Kluwer I took GPT-based and Azure Cognitive Services products from discovery to global deployment, and delivered DevBot, an internal chatbot grounded in company knowledge with retrieval-augmented generation and a vector database.

  • Automated ETL and Power BI reporting for Finance on Azure Databricks
  • DevSecOps scanning rolled out with Security and Legal
20% faster time to market 30% revenue growth 50% shorter reporting cycles
